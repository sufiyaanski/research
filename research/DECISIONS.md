# Decisions — motion toolkit (branch `motion-toolkit-2026-11-02`)

Each entry: what was decided, and why. Newest at the bottom.

1. **Decided: branch `motion-toolkit-2026-11-02` from `claude/tender-turing-2464tr`, not from `main`** (because that branch already holds the research files 00–05, the Remotion skills and the 3D blocky-character rig the toolkit reuses; `main` has only a README). Nothing is merged to main.
2. **Decided: the new toolkit lives in `toolkit/` as its own npm project, separate from `setup/`** (because the new spec differs from the older kit: 30 fps instead of 60, cream ground #F4EFE6 instead of the dark Signal palette, a blocky person in every Signal beat. Keeping them apart avoids breaking the older kit's rendered demos). `toolkit/HARNESS.md` is the authority for new reels; where it conflicts with `setup/CLAUDE.md`, HARNESS.md wins.
3. **Decided: YouTube access only via the routes the brief lists, used plainly** (because on 2026-11-02 the cloud IP got "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot" from yt-dlp and `IpBlocked` from youtube-transcript-api, and an attempt to try alternative player clients was refused as bot-check circumvention). So: no client rotation, no cookies, no third-party transcript proxies. Fallbacks in order: plain yt-dlp → youtube-transcript-api → public page/oEmbed/description → web write-ups. Every claim not confirmed from a transcript is marked [U] in `research/videos/`.
4. **Decided: captions block centred at y ≈ 1180 (inside y 1080–1280)** (because "lower-middle third" must also clear the platform UI: Meta's published bottom 35% (y > 1248) and the right action rail; 1180 is the lowest centre that keeps a 2-line 64–72 px block above that zone).
5. **Decided: entrances 8–12 frames, exits 40–60% of the entrance, at 30 fps** (because HyperFrames/LottieFiles measured 0.3–0.6 s entrances for premium motion, and the brief bans wobble; 8–12 f at 30 fps = 0.27–0.4 s keeps the faster short-form pace the reference creators use).
6. **Decided: the same original 3D blocky character (from `setup/remotion/src/lib/character/`) for both channels, re-coloured per channel** (because it already renders deterministically at 1080×1920 in Remotion, is original (no Minecraft/Roblox assets), and the brief asks for the same character on both channels). 3D needs `--gl=swangle` on GPU-less machines.
7. **Decided: code-synthesised SFX and music bed only (Node, no deps), committed as WAVs** (because the brief bans paid APIs, and synthesised sounds are ours with no licence questions). Paid/hosted tools seen in the tutorials (Claude Motion, Pixels MCP, Higgsfield, Fish Audio) are documented, not used.
8. **Decided: commit the example MP4s under `toolkit/out/`** (because the brief asks for rendered examples, and 12–15 s at 30 fps is a few MB each).
9. **Decided: keep the brief's critique (middle frame of every shot; story/composition/motion/type/sync; fix 3 worst; ≥ 8; max 3 rounds) and fold the source method's seven axes into the five definitions** (because the only video that shows the loop (Lukas Margerie, via the Movez course) uses one frame per *beat* and seven axes, and asks for *at least* 3 rounds; the brief's per-shot middle frame maps cleanly onto our shot list, and a max of 3 rounds keeps cost bounded. The Movez hunt list, loop-seam check and determinism hash check were added to HARNESS §4).
10. **Decided: no springs with overshoot, despite the source method using springs (k=170, d=26; tiny UI overshoot)** (because the brief says "no wobble unless asked"; cubic eases are the house default).
11. **Decided: the "crew" line names six roles (director, writer, animator, composer, sound designer, render engineer)** (because the brief asks for it; the source course names only four, with no writer or composer, so this is our extension, not a confirmed creator method).
12. **Decided: don't commit the user-supplied reference reel or frames from it; commit only our measurements and analysis** (because the video belongs to @analyst_dnb; our measured timeline, colours and format notes are enough to recreate the *format* with original content).
13. **Decided: analyse the reel by measurement — OCR of every on-screen text at 0.25 s (RapidOCR), Whisper for speech, ebur128 loudness, a spectrogram and colour sampling** (because "the exact formula" needs exact timings; eyeballing a contact sheet would miss the 11× slowed clock and the subtitle rhythm). Tool attribution ("code-rendered 3D") is an inference and is marked [U].
