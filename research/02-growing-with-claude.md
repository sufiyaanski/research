# Growing AI-Made Short-Form Channels: What the Evidence Says (research/02-growing-with-claude.md)

> Research date: 2026-11-02. Scope: Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, for two code- and AI-made channels: **The Signal** (Remotion motion graphics about price psychology, 45–70 s) and **Q-ROSITY** (blocky-character "what if" POV reels).
>
> **How to read this:**
> - "(unverified)" means I saw the claim only in a search snippet or second-hand and did not open the source.
> - "(vendor)" means the source sells a tool or course that benefits from the claim.
> - Platform statements outrank everything else here, and a lot of the "data" online is vendor content.
>
> **Access limits in this session:**
> - Reddit blocks this crawler, so I could not read any Reddit threads.
> - YouTube blocked transcript and subtitle downloads (IP block and bot check), so I could not read creator videos, including Millie Adrian's 75-trial-reel experiment. I only confirmed its title and channel through oEmbed.

## TL;DR: the 12 things most worth acting on

1. **On Instagram, sends and watch time drive reach to non-followers.** Mosseri (Jan 2025) named the top three ranking signals as **watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach**. He said sends are "slightly more important for unconnected content", meaning people who don't follow you. In Sept 2026 he named **originality, recency and testing small creators on non-followers** as ranking principles. ([SMT Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/), [SMT Sept 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/))
2. **On Shorts, the first gate is "Viewed vs. swiped away."** In Paddy Galloway's 2023 sample (5,400 Shorts, 3.3B views), Shorts below **60% viewed rarely did well**, and top Shorts sat at **70–90%**. YouTube publishes no official threshold. ([thread](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1646898356419981315.html))
3. **On Instagram, the matching gate is the 3-second "skip rate."** Instagram added it in Aug 2025 (it replaced "view rate") together with a per-reel retention chart. Instagram has published no benchmark. ([Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-retention-chart-skip-rate-new-performance-metrics-reels-9730992))
4. **Trial Reels are Instagram's built-in way to test on non-followers.** Mechanics:
   - Followers don't see them, and metrics arrive after about 24 h.
   - You can auto-share to followers if views do well in the first 72 h.
   - You need ≥1,000 followers and a public account.
   - Mosseri (Oct 2026, second-hand) said several *different* trials a day are fine, but reposting the *same* trial repeatedly "is not okay."

   Sources: [IG official](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels), [SMT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-access-trial-reels/752895/), [ZoomSphere](https://www.zoomsphere.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-actually-gets-reach).
5. **Posting more raises your odds of an outlier, not your typical result.**
   - Buffer, TikTok (11.4M posts): 11+ posts a week gave +34% views per post and raised the top-10% outcome (p90) from 3,722 to 14,401. The median was flat (489 → 459).
   - Buffer, Instagram (2.1M posts): follower growth rose with each step up in frequency, up to 10+ posts a week.

   Sources: [SEJ/Buffer TikTok](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/study-shows-2-5-weekly-tiktoks-deliver-biggest-view-increase/558641/), [Buffer IG](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/).
6. **Best length differs by platform.**
   - **Reels:** 45–60 s had the highest average views (Socialinsider, H1 2026, 9.8M Reels).
   - **TikTok:** longer does better. Buffer found >60 s gets 43% more reach than 30–60 s, and Socialinsider's 2026 average views peak at 120–180 s.
   - **Shorts:** the data conflicts. Pooled medians favour 11–30 s.

   Sources: [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/social-media-video-statistics), [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/longer-tiktoks-get-more-views-data/), [quso](https://quso.ai/research/youtube-shorts-length), [OverseerOS](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/best-youtube-shorts-length).
7. **YouTube's July 2025 "inauthentic content" policy hits Q-ROSITY's format directly.** Two items are explicitly not monetizable:
   - "Videos where characters are put in the same situation over and over again with the same outcome."
   - "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production."

   But "a series following a set of characters across episodes" is explicitly allowed. ([YouTube policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392))
8. **YouTube's spam policy covers the AI-volume risk.** It bans "using automated tools or AI to churn out high volumes of similar content with minimal changes." Its own example: "the exact same background music and repetitive AI generated imagery across many videos." It also says "posting a few variations of a video is ok." ([YouTube spam policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973))
9. **Shorts over 1 minute with any Content ID claim are blocked globally.** They can't be watched, recommended or monetized. That matters for 61–70 s Signal cuts that use licensed music. ([YouTube help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en))
10. **AI labels:**
    - **YouTube:** unrealistic or animated content doesn't need disclosure, and disclosing "doesn't limit a video's audience or affect monetization." ([YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491))
    - **TikTok:** label realistic AIGC; unlabeled realistic AIGC "may be removed, restricted, or labeled." ([TikTok CG](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity))
    - **Instagram:** since 31 Aug 2026, *profiles featuring an AI-generated person* must use the "AI-generated profile" label or lose recommendations. ([Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-to-limit-the-reach-of-unlabeled-ai-generated-profiles/))
11. **Comment-keyword CTAs reliably add comments. Nobody has shown they add reach.** Metricool found posts with comment-focused CTAs got **+202.78% comments** (correlation). Instagram lists "clickbait or engagement bait" as **not eligible for recommendation**. Meta's API allows **one** automated private reply per comment, within **7 days**. ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/), [IG originality](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality), [Meta dev docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/instagram-messaging/features/private-replies.md))
12. **Cross-posting your own video is fine if the file is clean.** Instagram requires "no visible watermarks" for recommendation eligibility. YouTube's Oct 2026 originality update targets re-uploads of *other creators'* content. TikTok makes "unoriginal or reused material without anything new" ineligible for the For You feed. ([IG](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality), [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/re-uploaded-shorts-lose-reach-as-youtube-favours-original-clips/), [TikTok CG](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity))

## 1. Hooks (first 1–3 seconds) and retention benchmarks

### 1.1 What the platforms measure

| Platform | Hook gate metric | Official definition / status | Benchmark evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube Shorts | **Viewed vs. swiped away** (now also "Stayed to watch") | Share of feed impressions where the viewer chose to watch rather than swipe. YouTube publishes no "good" cutoff. | Galloway 2023 (5,400 Shorts / 33 channels / 3.3B views): **<60% rarely performed; best 70–90%**; a high % didn't guarantee success. ([thread](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1646898356419981315.html)). Third-party guides say "aim for 70–80%" (unverified, no source). |
| YouTube Shorts | **Engaged views** | Since 31 Mar 2025, public Shorts views count every start or replay. "Engaged views" is the old stricter count and still drives YPP eligibility and earnings. On 24 Aug 2026 the start-counting rule extended to long-form and live. ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-changes-how-it-counts-views-on-long-form-live/586166/)) | Compare videos on **engaged views**, not public views, especially across the Mar 2025 and Aug 2026 cutovers. |
| Instagram Reels | **Skip rate** (% of views that swiped away within 3 s) + **Retention chart** | Announced Aug 2025, replacing "view rate." Instagram's guidance: "the flatter the line, the more engaged your audience." ([Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-retention-chart-skip-rate-new-performance-metrics-reels-9730992)) | **No official benchmark.** Metricool says so explicitly and gives only qualitative tiers ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-reel-analytics/)). "30–40% healthy / >50% throttled" circulates but is unsourced (unverified). |
| Instagram | Avg watch time, likes/reach, sends/reach | Mosseri, Jan 2025: "pay close attention to average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach." ([SMT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)) | Metricool 2026 (24.4M posts): **average Reel watch time 8.5 s**, more than double year over year ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/)). This is a useful baseline. |
| TikTok | Watch time / completion (no public hook metric) | TikTok ads research: **>63% of highest-CTR ads show the key message in the first 3 s**; 40% of the highest view-through-rate ads use text overlays; vertical 9:16 gives **25% higher 6-second watch-through rate**. ([TikTok for Business](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/9-creative-tips-to-drive-auction-ad-performance)) | This is ad data, correlational, with no sample or method given. It is directional only for organic posts. |

### 1.2 Hook patterns that fit these two formats

**Honest caveat:** I found **no independent, controlled study** comparing hook types for explainer or curiosity content. What exists:

- **Front-load the payoff or claim.** TikTok's 63% stat above, and YouTube's Todd Sherman (2023): Shorts get a "seed audience" and expand or taper based on whether people watch or swipe ([OutlierKit summary of Creator Insider Q&A](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)). Galloway (2023, unverified secondary) calls Shorts "like intros, except they need to have a conclusion."
- **Hook in under 4 s, stating the whole prize up front.** One Claude/Remotion reel creator (FreshStack) says this pattern took an account from **1,500 to 6,000+ followers in 4 weeks** at 5 reels a week, with best reels at **200K and 100K views**. They also lock the layout (title, captions and face in fixed positions) and put text inside Instagram's safe zone (top 220 px, bottom 450 px). Self-reported and promotional (vendor). ([FreshStack](https://www.freshstack.ai/resources/automate-instagram-reels-with-claude))
- **Animated explainer skeleton.** Example account **@rick.theengineer**: 108K followers, top reels at 607K / 749K / 954K views, word-by-word captions, one idea per frame. Recommended structure: hook 0–3 s, setup 3–15 s, mechanism 15–60 s, payoff in the last 5–10 s, with hooks that "name a mystery or gap rather than announcing the topic." (Vendor post; account stats are a snapshot, and the timings are the author's advice, not measured.) ([Brainrot Shorts](https://www.brainrotshorts.com/blog/how-to-make-animated-explainer-reels))
- **Kallaway's "context → scroll-stop interjection → contrarian snapback"** (curiosity loop through contrast), with visual, text and spoken hooks all aligned. (Unverified; only third-party summaries found.)
- **Vendor numbers to ignore or treat as weak:**
  - OpusClip says "50–60% of viewers who leave a Short do so in the first 3 seconds" (internal data, unverified).
  - A "Meta 2025 study: 65% who watch 3 s stay 10 s" (no primary source found).
  - "Question openings +22% retention" (unverified).

**Implications for the two channels:**
- **The Signal:** open on the *number* and the *contradiction* in frame 1. Example: "$9.99 vs $10. One of these made 24% more sales." Animated number on screen, first word spoken within 0.3 s, no logo intro. A recurring intro is *allowed* by YouTube only if "the bulk of your content is different."
- **Q-ROSITY:** open on the *stakes in POV*. Example: "POV: you wake up and gravity is 10× stronger" with the character already mid-reaction in frame 1. Write the "what if" as on-screen text in the first frame, because most viewers decide before audio registers.
- **Looping endings:** no platform statement says loops are rewarded. YouTube counts replays as views (since Mar 2025), and Instagram's "views" now include replays per Metricool/Agorapulse (conflicting: Brandwatch says replays aren't counted). A loop mainly inflates views and watch-time ratios. Use it on Q-ROSITY, where a cliffhanger can wrap back to the opening line, but there is no evidence it changes distribution.

## 2. Formats, series and length

### 2.1 Length: what the 2025–2026 data says

| Platform | Max length | Data | Takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram Reels** | 3 min (since ~20 Jan 2025, up from 90 s) (unverified, multiple news reports) | **Socialinsider, Jan–Jun 2026, 9.79M brand Reels (1K–1M followers):** avg views 0–30 s 4,700; 30–45 s 8,564; **45–60 s 10,374 (peak)**; 60–90 s 9,790; 90–120 s 8,000; 120–180 s 9,000; >180 s 4,428. Engagement rate also peaks at 45–60 s (0.35%). ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/social-media-video-statistics)) | The Signal's 45–70 s range is right in the best band. Keep Reels **≤60–65 s**. |
| **TikTok** | 10 min upload (60 min in some places) | **Buffer (Mar 2025, ~1.1M TikToks):** >60 s gets **43.2% more reach** than 30–60 s and 63.8% more watch time ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/longer-tiktoks-get-more-views-data/)). **Socialinsider 2026 (8.0M TikToks):** avg views 45–60 s 3,300 → 60–90 s 8,000 → **120–180 s 12,000**. **Counterpoint, Revid (2.39M already-popular TikToks):** median views peak at 16–30 s (141K) ([Revid](https://www.revid.ai/short-form-video-statistics)). | TikTok rewards >60 s for typical accounts. **Creator Rewards needs videos >1 min**, plus 10K followers and 100K views in 30 days (unverified, third-party guides consistent). Make TikTok cuts **61–80 s**. |
| **YouTube Shorts** | 3 min (since 15 Oct 2024, square/vertical) ([YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en)) | **quso.ai (108K matured Shorts, vendor):** medians 11–20 s **901**; 21–30 s 542; 46–60 s 188; 61–90 s 186 ([quso](https://quso.ai/research/youtube-shorts-length)). **OverseerOS (16,537 Shorts, Sep–Oct 2026):** pooled median best at 16–30 s (36,970). Within channels, 16–30 s beat 31–60 s on only **57%** of channels (ratio 1.09×), and on a channel-balanced basis **121–180 s ranked top** ([OverseerOS](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/best-youtube-shorts-length)). **Galloway 2023:** longer Shorts did better and AVD >50 s averaged 4.1M views (pre-3-min era). Sherman: "To what length do you need to tell your story?" | Evidence conflicts. Shorter usually means higher completion. **Test a 30–45 s Shorts cut of each Signal piece against the full cut.** Over 60 s, use only YouTube Audio Library or original music, because any Content ID claim blocks a >1 min Short globally. |

### 2.2 Series, recurring characters, numbered episodes

- **YouTube explicitly allows** "Similar content, like a series following a set of characters across episodes" and "Same intro and outro … but the bulk of your content is different."
- **YouTube explicitly prohibits** (no monetization) "Videos where characters are put in the same situation over and over again with the same outcome." Also prohibited under "Unsatisfying or Off-putting Content": "content that lacks a clear narrative arc," "channels that heavily rely on generic templates or emotionally manipulative themes," and repeated disturbing themes without a cohesive narrative. ([YouTube monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392))
  - **For Q-ROSITY this is the biggest single policy risk.** If every episode is "blocky guy faces X, panics, dies," it fits the prohibited pattern word for word. Vary the outcomes, give episodes an arc (callbacks, a running goal), and give the character a consistent personality.
- **Evidence that "Part 1/2/3" lifts follows:** I found **no measured data**. There are only practitioner claims, e.g. a viewer who goes to the profile for Part 2 is "a powerful signal" ([Tokime](https://tokimepartners.com/guides/part-1-part-2-series-clips)) (unverified).
  - Caution: TikTok's guidelines make "content that tries to manipulate people into … inflating likes or follows" ineligible for the For You feed ([TikTok CG](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity)). "Follow for part 2" is common, but it is technically in that zone. Prefer "Part 2 is up on my page."
- **Faceless curiosity analogue: Zack D. Films** (animated curiosity Shorts, roughly 1 min). Started 7 Jun 2021; breakout Toyota Short on 11 Jun 2022 (7.2M views). Milestones: 1M subs Aug 2022 → 5M Sep 2023 → 10M Apr 2024 → 11M May 2024 ([Guidesify](https://opinion.guidesify.com/zack-d-films-the-man-who-explains-various-random-interesting-topics-across-the-internet/)). About 25M by Dec 2025 and about 29M by late Oct 2026, gaining roughly 800K in October (unverified, trackers). **Takeaway:** about a year of output before the first breakout, then compounding. It is a consistent single-visual-style, one-question-per-Short format.
- **Other "what if" or faceless explainers** (OutlierKit snapshot, no timelines): What If 8.9M subs (long-form); AlternateHistoryHub 2M+ ([OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/faceless-youtube-growth-strategy/), [BecomeViral](https://becomeviral.com/blog/top-faceless-youtube-channel-case-studies-2026), the latter a course seller).
- **AI recurring-character virality:** "Italian brainrot" (Tralalero Tralala, Jan 2025) went from TikTok AI characters into Roblox ("Steal a Brainrot") and merchandise. Hashtag views are claimed at 3.7B and 5.3B (unverified, third-party analytics). It shows that **named, recurring, weird characters** can spread across platforms. It is also the "brainrot" category that Kapwing found makes up 33% of a new account's Shorts feed, and that YouTube is now actively demoting and removing (see §6).

### 2.3 Format mix notes

- Metricool 2026 (Instagram): **carousels get 9× the saves of single images and 4.7× the views of TikTok carousels**. **Posts with ≥1 hashtag got 31.7% fewer views** (correlation). Most views arrive **within 3 days**. ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/))
- Instagram caps hashtags at **5 per post/Reel** (Mosseri, Dec 2025). He also says hashtags help search, not reach (unverified, many news reports, e.g. [SMT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagrams-implementing-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)).
- The Signal's data-heavy topics are natural **carousel companions**: post the reel, then a carousel version two days later. Carousels earn saves and sends. (Mosseri, Oct 2026 AMA, second-hand: carousels "tend to do better than single photos.")

## 3. Posting cadence and cross-posting

### 3.1 Frequency evidence

| Source | Sample | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| **Buffer – Instagram** (Aug 2025) | ~2.1M posts, ~102K accounts, within-account fixed effects | Follower-growth lift vs no posting: 1–2/wk **+0.12%**, 3–5/wk **+0.26%**, 6–9/wk **+0.44%**, 10+/wk **+0.66%**. Median reach per post vs 1–2/wk: +12% (3–5), +18% (6–9), +24% (10+). Weeks with no posts sit 0.08 SD below baseline. Diminishing returns; 6–9 and 10+ flagged "treat with caution." ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)) |
| **Buffer – TikTok** (Oct 2025) | 11.4M posts, 150K+ accounts, fixed effects | Views per post vs 1/wk: 2–5/wk **+17%**, 6–10 **+29%**, 11+ **+34%**. Median roughly flat (489 → 459). **p90: 3,722 → 14,401.** More posts mostly means more shots at a hit. ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/study-shows-2-5-weekly-tiktoks-deliver-biggest-view-increase/558641/)) |
| **Metricool – YouTube** (2026) | 799,718 videos, 71,177 accounts | Per-Short performance best at **2–4 Shorts a week**; no monthly ceiling on total views. Shorts views +127% YoY, but **average view duration fell ~67% to ~16 s**. ~5 Shorts/month correlated with +46% long-form views; 35+/month erased that lift ("correlation isn't causation"). ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/youtube-trends/)) |
| **Mosseri** (Sept 2026) | — | "Post as much as you can to improve your odds of a hit," while avoiding burnout. ([SMT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/)) |
| **Sherman, YouTube** (2023) | — | No daily quota or hidden threshold. Time of day is "not something that we think about optimizing for." Deleting and re-posting "gets seen as spam." ([OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)) |
| Vendor: FacelessGenie | 412 faceless accounts in its own network, Mar–May 2026 | Avg **11,400 new followers in 90 days** at ≥3 reels/wk; those passing 100K posted 3–5/wk. **Vendor data, no method disclosed.** ([FacelessGenie](https://www.facelessgenie.ai/blog/faceless-reels)) |

**1 per day vs 3 per day:** no study isolates this for faceless or AI accounts. Buffer's data says more posts raise the ceiling with flat medians, so 3 a day helps *only* if quality holds. YouTube's spam policy adds a real downside for AI channels flooding near-identical content. **Recommendation:** 1 public post per day per platform, plus extra hook variants as Instagram Trial Reels rather than extra public posts.

### 3.2 Cross-posting the same video

- **Instagram:** recommendation eligibility requires content to be original, with **no visible watermarks** ([IG](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)). Mosseri (Oct 2026 AMA, second-hand): "We don't demote content for being created with other apps … as long as they don't have watermarks." Upload the **clean Remotion render**, never a TikTok download.
- **YouTube (1 Oct 2026):** Shorts will "further prioritize original content and reduce the reach of content re-uploaded from other creators." The target is channels "primarily aggregating or re-uploading other creators' videos." YouTube warns against "minor technical edits or template-based changes simply to alter a video" ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/re-uploaded-shorts-lose-reach-as-youtube-favours-original-clips/)). Your own content isn't the target.
- **TikTok:** FYF-ineligible if "unoriginal or reused material without anything new." Your own originals qualify.
- **Instagram aggregators (30 Apr 2026):** accounts that mainly repost others are now ineligible for recommendations across photos and carousels too. "Materially edited" counts as original; watermarks or speed changes don't ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)).
- **Practical rule:** same master file, platform-specific captions, and **platform-specific lengths** (§2.1). For TikTok, consider a native on-screen text style and trending-sound layer, since TikTok ads data credits text overlays and sound.

## 4. Instagram Trial Reels and other testing tools

### 4.1 What they are (official)

- Launched **10 Dec 2024**. Trial reels are "shown to non-followers first." They don't appear in followers' feeds, your Reels tab or your grid unless you share them later.
- You turn on the **"Trial" toggle** before sharing.
- After about **24 h** you see views, likes, comments and shares, plus a **comparison with your previous trials**.
- You can share to everyone manually, or set **auto-share "if it performs well based on views in the first 72 hours"** ([Instagram](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)).
- **Eligibility:** ≥1,000 followers, public account (Mosseri via [SMT, Jul 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-access-trial-reels/752895/)). You can schedule them through Later and similar tools.

### 4.2 Rules from Mosseri (second-hand, Sept–Oct 2026)

- Experimenting with Trial Reels is fine: "It's not going to affect your account in a negative way."
- Reposting the same Trial Reel repeatedly "is not okay. We're going to crack down on that." (Also reported as "can be identified by Instagram" by [The Reel Stars, Sept 2026](https://www.thereelstars.com/tech/adam-mosseri-reveals-instagram-trial-reels-rule-that-could-limit-your-reach/).)
- Several *different* trial reels in one day are "totally fine."

Source: [ZoomSphere](https://www.zoomsphere.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-actually-gets-reach). The quotes come from a secondary blog; I could not open the AMA itself.

**Implication:** vary the **hook, edit, pacing, text, VO or ending** between variants, not just re-upload.

### 4.3 Numbers (all weak)

- **CreatorHouse 14-day test** (vendor; 4,200-follower account; 10 trial vs 10 normal ~15 s reels, Apr 2026) ([CreatorHouse](https://creatorhouse.app/blog/trial-reels-14-day-test-2026)):

  | Metric | Trial reels | Normal reels |
  |---|---|---|
  | Median reach | 14,800 | 6,400 (2.3×) |
  | Non-follower reach | 96% | 62% |
  | Like/view | 1.4% | 4.1% |
  | Save/view | 0.4% | 1.7% |
  | Comment/view | 0.2% | 1.1% |
  | Net follows per reel | 12.4 | 8.8 (1.4×) |

  Their "share if >2× normal reach in 24 h" rule has no supporting data. The article's own "0.6× engagement" headline contradicts its table.
- **Millie Adrian ("Modern Millie"), "I posted 75 Instagram Trial Reels in 15 days"** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijGh9LtBJ7U); I confirmed title and channel only). A secondary write-up says she gained about **2,600 followers**, with about **90% from just two talking-head videos** that had already worked organically ([Creatorsjet](https://www.creatorsjet.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)). Volume found a couple of winners; most trials did little.
- **Mosseri** (second-hand, unverified): trials "almost always get less reach than regular Reels" because they lack the follower boost. This contradicts CreatorHouse.
- **FacelessGenie** (vendor): hit rate about 18% on first-try reels vs about **64% after trial validation**. Promotion threshold ≥1.4× account average reach and ≥55% watch time. No method disclosed.
- **Later (Mar 2026):** test hooks (clarity vs curiosity), re-test old winners on cold audiences, test sounds. Run 1–2 experiments a week, tag variants, compare in grouped analytics ([Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels-strategy-how-to-use/)). No outcome data.

### 4.4 Ajené Pinnock

I found **no primary trial-reels tutorial** from Ajené Pinnock: his YouTube was not readable from here and searches returned nothing. ## 5. Comment-keyword CTAs and ManyChat-style automation

**Platform rules:**
- **Instagram** lists "**Clickbait or engagement bait**" and "**Low-quality, repurposed content lacking added value**" as not eligible for recommendations ([IG](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)).
- **Meta's Facebook distribution guidelines** define engagement bait as "Posts that explicitly request engagement (such as votes, shares, comments, tags, likes, or other reactions)" for purposes other than a specific call to action. Exceptions are things like missing people or petitions ([Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/content-distribution-guidelines/engagement-bait)). The 2017–2019 history: Meta demoted "comment bait" ("Comment YES if…"), extended it to comments (2018) and to video audio (2019) (unverified, news coverage).
- **Meta API limits** for comment-to-DM tools: **1 private reply per comment, within 7 days**; follow-ups only after the user replies, inside 24 h ([Meta dev](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/instagram-messaging/features/private-replies.md)). A rate limit of about 750 calls/hour/account was reported (unverified).
- **TikTok:** FYF-ineligible if it "tries to manipulate people into giving gifts or inflating likes or follows" ([TikTok CG](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity)).

**Evidence on effect:**
- **Comments:** Metricool 2026: comment-focused CTAs **+202.78% comments**; posts with a question **+36.70% comments** ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/)). Correlational.
- **Reach:** **no independent evidence either way.** Mosseri's named signals are watch time, likes and sends, not comments. A vendor (Creator Lane) argues that a bot DM to a commenter does **not** count as a "send" of the reel (unverified).
- **ManyChat case "Roma by Rochi"** (vendor): **+741% engagement, +82% reach, 43K comments, 4,470 emails, 6,000+ followers in one week**. But it was a **giveaway** with story-mention bonus entries, so it isn't attributable to keyword DMs alone ([CaseStudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/manychat/case-study/how-roma-by-rochi-saw-a-741-lift-in-engagement-using-instagram-automation)).
- **Vendors running it on Claude-made reels:** FreshStack (ManyChat keyword gate to deliver links; 1.5K → 6K followers in 4 weeks, self-reported). Ootto (claims "10,000+ leads in a month," one reel with 5,261 comments; vendor, no baseline).

**Bottom line:** a keyword CTA is a **lead-capture and comment-velocity tool**, not a proven reach tool, and it sits close to Instagram's "engagement bait" exclusion. Use it when you have a real deliverable, keep it short, and A/B it with Trial Reels.

## 6. Platform policies on AI content (what can hurt an AI-made channel)

### 6.1 YouTube

- **15 Jul 2025:** the "repetitious content" policy was renamed "**inauthentic content**." YouTube's changelog calls it a "minor update" clarifying it covers "repetitive or mass-produced" content; the reused-content policy is unchanged ([YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)). Rene Ritchie also called it a "minor update" (unverified, mirrored news).
- **Current policy headings** (verified Nov 2026), relevant items:
  - *Generic or Repetitive (not allowed):* low educational value or minimal variation; **same situation/same outcome characters**; slideshows or scrolling text with minimal narrative; **AI content from generic templates giving the impression of mass production**.
  - *Unsatisfying or Off-putting:* no clear narrative arc; generic templates or emotionally manipulative themes.
  - *AI Personas Related to Sensitive Topics (not allowed):* AI doctor; **"AI-generated podcast hosts offering financial guidance, investment tips, or wealth management advice"**; AI legal advice. **The Signal should stay on pricing and consumer psychology, cite sources, and not present an AI persona giving investment or money advice.**
- **Spam policy item 6, "Automated or synthetic mass-production":** prohibits high volumes of similar AI content with minimal changes. Example: "exact same background music and repetitive AI generated imagery across many videos." "Posting a few variations of a video is ok." Disclosure "is not a free pass" ([YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973)).
- **AI disclosure:** required for **realistic** synthetic content (real people, real events, realistic scenes, AI music as focus). **Not required** for unrealistic or animated content, cosmetic edits, cloning your own voice, or AI help with scripts, titles or ideas. Disclosure "doesn't limit a video's audience or affect its monetization." For animated or non-photoreal content the label shows in the expanded description ([YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)). **Both channels are non-realistic, so disclosure is likely not required** (see §9 for TTS voices).
- **Enforcement reality:**
  - **Kapwing (Nov 2025):** **21% (104/500)** of a fresh account's first 500 Shorts were AI slop and 33% "brainrot"; the top slop channel (Cuentos Facinantes) had 5.95M subs ([Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/blog/ai-slop-report-the-global-rise-of-low-quality-ai-videos/)).
  - **Jan 2026:** after Neal Mohan's letter ("reduce the spread of low quality AI content … building on our established systems"), at least 16–18 top slop channels were deleted or emptied, including Cuentos Facinantes (>1.2B views) ([Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2026/01/29/youtube-ai-slop-channel-crackdown-bans/)).
  - **1 Oct 2026:** Shorts originality ranking update (above).
- **YPP rejections:** creator anecdotes of "reused content" rejections even for original AI films exist (unverified, Reddit cited second-hand). Faceless isn't disqualifying; sameness is.

### 6.2 Instagram / Meta

- **Originality** (2024, expanded 30 Apr 2026): only originals are recommended when duplicates exist; repeated reposters become ineligible; aggregators lose recommendations across formats ([IG](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)). Your own Remotion animations are original.
- **AI labels:**
  - **Post-level "AI info"** is triggered by C2PA/SynthID metadata or self-disclosure. No reach penalty has been established (unverified).
  - **Account-level "AI-generated profile"** (renamed 31 Aug 2026): for profiles "built around a synthetic person" "generated or substantially created with AI." Unlabeled detected profiles **lose recommendations in Reels and Explore**; labeling proactively causes "no change." Creators who "simply use AI tools as part of their creative process don't need to add the label" ([Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-to-limit-the-reach-of-unlabeled-ai-generated-profiles/)).
  - Whether a **non-human blocky cartoon character** counts as an "AI-generated person" is **not addressed**. It is probably not covered, but see §9.
- **Mosseri, Oct 2026 AMA** (second-hand): "We do not throttle reach of AI content just for being AI content … as long as it's content that you're interested in and that it's labeled."
- **Mosseri, end of 2025:** "authenticity is fast becoming a scarce resource"; he predicts a shift toward raw, imperfect aesthetics. Implication: give the AI channels a distinctive *point of view* and recognisable craft, not generic polish.

### 6.3 TikTok

- Must label "AI-generated or significantly edited content that shows **realistic-looking** scenes or people." Unlabeled content "may be removed, restricted, or labeled."
- "Unoriginal or reused material without anything new" is FYF-ineligible.
- "Automation tools, scripts, or other tricks designed to bypass our systems" are banned. That covers engagement manipulation, not code-made video ([TikTok CG](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity)).
- 1.3B+ videos labeled so far; invisible watermarking (tests); a viewer **AIGC slider** in Manage Topics ([TikTok newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/more-ways-to-spot-shape-and-understand-ai-content?lang=en)).
- **Creator Rewards and AI:** sources conflict, from "fully AI-generated content excluded" to "no AI ban" (all unverified third-party). Check the in-app eligibility screen.

### 6.4 How to stay safe (checklist)

1. **Variation by design.** Rotate background music, colour accents and scene layouts per episode. Never ship two videos with the same structure and different numbers only. Q-ROSITY outcomes must vary.
2. **Visible human authorship.** Original research and sources for The Signal (show the study and year on screen). A distinct narrator persona and catchphrases. Real commentary ("here's why this works on you").
3. **No AI persona giving financial or investment advice.** Frame The Signal as how sellers price, not what to buy.
4. **Label where realistic.** Neither channel is photoreal, so labels are likely optional. If any realistic AI footage or voice of a real person appears, label it on all three platforms.
5. **Clean masters, no watermarks.** Don't delete and re-upload on YouTube. Don't re-post identical Trial Reels.
6. **Audio:** for any Short over 60 s, use only original or YouTube Audio Library music.

## 8. The analytics loop: what to watch and when to double down

**Rule zero:** benchmark against **your own medians**. None of the three platforms publishes "good" thresholds, and most circulating numbers are unsourced.

| Platform | Primary (hook) | Secondary (hold) | Growth (spread) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube Shorts | **Viewed vs swiped away** (aim ≥70%; <60% means a hook problem, per Galloway 2023) | Avg % viewed / retention curve; **engaged views** | Subs gained per 1K engaged views; shares | Compare on engaged views. Public views have been inflated since Mar 2025 (Shorts) and Aug 2026 (all formats). Most organic Shorts views (>61%) come from the Shorts feed (Metricool). |
| Instagram Reels | **Skip rate** (first 3 s) | Avg watch time vs **8.5 s platform average** (Metricool 2026); retention chart flatness | **Sends per reach**, follows per reach, likes per reach | Use Edits app Insights → Compare (up to 3 reels: views, watch time, follows, interactions) ([Edits](https://edits.instagram.com/blog/video-insights/)). Trial reels: compare against your previous trials in-app. |
| TikTok | 2-second / early retention, average watch time | Completion rate; % watched full video | Shares, profile visits → follows, FYF % of views | ">70% completion needed for virality" is unsourced (unverified). |

**Suggested decision thresholds** (heuristics I'm proposing, not platform rules; tune after 30 posts):
- **Kill or rework the hook** if, after 24–48 h, YouTube viewed-vs-swiped is under 60%, or Instagram skip rate is worse than your 30-day median by more than 10 points.
- **Rework the middle** if the hook metric is fine but the retention curve drops more than 25 points between 3 s and 50% of runtime.
- **Double down (make 3 follow-ups in the same format)** if a post hits **≥2× your 30-day median** on views from non-followers *and* is at or above median on sends per reach (IG) or subs per 1K engaged views (YT).
- **Promote a trial reel** if after 24 h it is in the **top third of your previous trials** (Instagram shows the comparison) *and* its skip rate is at or below your median. Otherwise leave it as a trial, or use auto-share and let Instagram's 72-hour view rule decide.
- Sends per reach: Creator Lane's "1–2% strong, 3%+ viral" is vendor folklore with no sample (unverified). Use your own distribution.

## 9. What to do for The Signal and Q-ROSITY: a concrete weekly plan

### 9.1 Production specs (both channels)

- Master render 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264. No platform watermark. Captions inside the safe zone (top ~220 px, bottom ~450 px, right-hand buttons clear).
- **Platform cuts from one Remotion composition** (parametrise duration):
  - **The Signal:** IG Reels **50–60 s**. TikTok **62–75 s** (crosses the 1-min mark for Creator Rewards; Buffer's >60 s reach lift). YouTube Shorts **two variants over time**: a 35–45 s "tight" cut and the 55–60 s cut, keeping YT ≤60 s unless the audio is original or Audio Library.
  - **Q-ROSITY:** IG and YT **30–45 s** episodes; TikTok **61–70 s** "extended episode" (or two-scene episodes) if chasing Creator Rewards.
- **Variation engine** to avoid the YouTube "mass-production" pattern: 4–6 music beds, 3 colour palettes and several scene templates rotated per episode. Each episode must have a *different outcome or insight*.

### 9.2 Cadence (per channel, per week)

| | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Public posts** (IG + TikTok + YT, same piece, native cuts) | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 (best-of / carousel for The Signal; "viewer-choice" episode for Q-ROSITY) | — (review) |
| **IG Trial Reels** (once ≥1,000 followers) | 2 hook variants of Tue's piece | 2 of Wed's | 2 of Thu's | 2 of Fri's | 2 of Sat's | 1–2 re-tests of last week's top public post with a new hook | — |

- About **6 public posts a week** (Buffer's 6–9/wk band on IG; the 6–10 band on TikTok gave +29% views per post).
- **8–12 trial reels a week**, each a genuinely different hook or edit (Mosseri: different trials per day are fine; identical repeats are not).
- **Before 1,000 IG followers** (no Trial Reels): post publicly 1×/day and use TikTok and Shorts as the testing ground.
- Sunday: analytics review (§9.5) and pick next week's double-downs.
- Don't exceed 1 public post per day per platform until the median holds. Extra volume goes into trials, not public posts.

### 9.3 Series ideas

**The Signal** (price psychology, numbers-first):
- *"The $0.01 Files"* (numbered #001…): charm pricing, left-digit effect, $9.99 vs $10 studies.
- *"Decoy Watch"*: one real menu or SaaS pricing page a week, the decoy circled, the maths animated.
- *"Receipt Autopsy"*: break down a real (public) receipt or price change: shrinkflation, unit price, anchoring.
- *"Price Check: 1990 vs Now"*: inflation-adjusted animated comparisons, which loop well.
- Every episode cites the study or source on screen (protects against "low educational value" and builds authority). Avoid "what you should invest in" framing (YouTube AI-persona and finance rule).

**Q-ROSITY** (what-if, blocky character, POV):
- *"What If Day N"*: a numbered daily series where the same character carries consequences forward (inventory, injuries, a running goal). This makes it a "series following characters across episodes," which is allowed, not "same situation, same outcome."
- *"You Chose This"*: Saturday episode built from top comment suggestions. It drives natural comments without "comment A or B" bait.
- *"Survive X Seconds"* with a visible timer: structure gives retention. **Rotate outcomes** (survive, fail, twist) and log them in a spreadsheet so no outcome repeats more than twice in a row.
- Two-parters only when the story needs it. End with "Part 2 is on my page," not "follow for part 2" (TikTok FYF wording risk).
- Keep the character visually original. Use no Minecraft or Roblox logos, names or assets (the IP risk wasn't researched here; check before monetizing).

### 9.4 Trial-reel testing protocol (Instagram)

1. **One variable per test.** Default variable = **hook** (first 2 s: on-screen text plus first spoken line). Secondary tests: opening visual, VO voice, length (45 vs 60 s), ending (loop vs CTA).
2. For each piece, generate **2 hook variants** in Remotion as separate compositions. Label them in your log (e.g. `SIG-014-HA`, `SIG-014-HB`).
3. Post both as Trial Reels within the same 2-hour window. Leave auto-share **off** for clean comparison.
4. **Read at 24 h** (metrics land then): views, skip rate, avg watch time, shares or sends, follows, and Instagram's "vs previous trials" comparison.
5. **Decide:**
   - **Winner** = lower skip rate *and* ≥1.3× the other variant's views. Share it to everyone (it stays on the grid) **and** port that hook to TikTok and Shorts.
   - **No clear winner** (<1.3× views or contradictory signals): publish the version with the better skip rate and log "inconclusive."
6. **Build a hook library:** after 20 tests, tally which hook *types* win: number-first, contradiction, POV stakes, question, "you've been tricked." Promote winners to the default template.
7. **Never re-upload the identical trial.** For a re-test, change the hook text, first frame and pacing at minimum.
8. **Monthly:** re-trial last month's top public post with a fresh hook on cold audiences (Later's "repurpose winners" tactic).

**YouTube and TikTok equivalent:** test the hook type *across days* (e.g. alternate number-first and question-first hooks) and compare viewed-vs-swiped (YT) and early retention (TT) per hook type over 10+ posts. Don't post near-duplicates the same day on YouTube.

### 9.5 CTA scripts (use on at most about 1 in 3 posts; A/B them via trials)

**The Signal (lead magnet, Instagram):**
- On screen, last 2 s: *"Want all 12 pricing tricks in one sheet? Comment **SIGNAL**."*
- Caption: *"Comment SIGNAL and I'll DM you the free cheat sheet (12 price-psychology tactics + the studies behind them)."*
- DM (one automated reply allowed per comment, sent within 7 days): *"Here's your Signal cheat sheet → [link]. Which trick surprised you most? Reply and I'll send the bonus one."* The question invites a reply, which opens the 24 h window for follow-ups.
- Non-bait alternative (preferred for reach): *"Send this to the friend who always falls for 'only $9.99'."* This targets **sends**, Mosseri's top signal for non-followers. It is still a request, so test it rather than assume.

**Q-ROSITY (conversation, no gate):**
- On screen: *"What would YOU have done differently?"* (Metricool: questions +36.7% comments.)
- Caption: *"Best answer becomes Saturday's episode."* A genuine reason to comment, not "comment 1 or 2."
- Avoid: "Like if…", "Comment YES", "Follow for part 2" (Meta and TikTok bait definitions).

### 9.6 Metrics thresholds and weekly review

- **Weekly dashboard per channel** (Metricool MCP, YT Studio and IG Insights): for each post, engaged views (YT), viewed-vs-swiped (YT), skip rate (IG), avg watch time (IG, TT), sends per reach (IG), follows per 1K views (all), comments (CTA posts vs non-CTA).
- **Go/no-go rules:** see §8. Additionally, if **CTA posts** show lower median non-follower reach than non-CTA posts over 4 weeks, cut CTAs to 1 in 5.
- **Format kill rule:** if a series has 6 consecutive episodes below the account's 30-day median on the hook metric, retire it or re-hook it.
- **Format scale rule:** any post at ≥2× median non-follower views gets 3 follow-ups in the same format within 7 days.
- **Policy health check (monthly):** scroll your last 10 uploads. If a stranger would think "same video, different numbers," vary harder (YouTube inauthentic and spam policies).

## 10. Uncertain or conflicting evidence

1. **Best Shorts length:** quso (11–20 s best) vs OverseerOS (16–30 s pooled, but 121–180 s best on a channel-balanced basis) vs Galloway 2023 (longer better). All are correlational, with different samples and view definitions (YouTube changed view counting in Mar 2025 and Aug 2026). **Test it yourself.**
2. **TikTok length:** Buffer and Socialinsider say longer gets more reach. Revid (already-popular TikToks only) says 16–30 s has the highest median. A selection effect probably explains the gap.
3. **Trial Reels reach:** CreatorHouse says trials get 2.3× reach; Mosseri (second-hand) says trials usually get *less* reach. Both are weak sources.
4. **Instagram "views" and replays:** Metricool and Agorapulse say views include replays since Apr 2025; Brandwatch says they don't.
5. **Skip-rate benchmarks** (30–40% "healthy," >50% "throttled") and **sends-per-reach benchmarks** (1–2% strong): no primary source. The claim that sends are weighted "3–5× likes" isn't confirmed by Meta.
6. **TikTok "48-hour follower-first test" (Oct 2026):** a single AI-drafted article with no official source. Unverified.
7. **TikTok Creator Rewards and AI:** third-party guides contradict each other on whether fully AI-generated videos qualify.
8. **Instagram "AI-generated profile" label scope:** the policy targets AI-generated *persons*. Whether a stylised blocky avatar counts is unspecified. Conservative option: if Q-ROSITY's character is presented as a "creator persona" (talking to camera as if real), consider the label. Mosseri (second-hand) says labeled AI content isn't throttled.
9. **AI voiceover disclosure:** YouTube exempts cloning *your own* voice. A generic TTS narrator that is clearly a narrator isn't "realistic depiction of a real person," so disclosure is likely not required. The policy text doesn't address stock TTS voices explicitly.
10. **Comment-keyword CTAs and reach:** vendors claim lifts; there are no independent tests, and Instagram excludes "engagement bait" from recommendations. Net effect unknown.
11. **Posting 1/day vs 3/day:** no direct study. Buffer shows a higher ceiling with a flat median. YouTube's spam policy adds risk for AI volume.
12. **Mosseri's Oct 2026 AMA quotes** (AI not throttled; different trials per day fine; scheduling doesn't hurt): reported by ZoomSphere only. I could not open the originals.
13. **Guru and vendor skepticism:** FacelessGenie, BecomeViral, Ootto, Creator Lane, CreatorHouse, Brainrot Shorts, FreshStack, OpusClip and ManyChat all sell the thing their data supports. The Remotion launch "6M views" vs "147K views" conflict shows how unreliable viral-claim numbers are.
14. **Research gaps:** Reddit, X and YouTube transcripts were unreachable, so community case studies (r/NewTubers, r/PartneredYoutube, r/ClaudeAI and others) and Ajené Pinnock's and Paddy Galloway's 2026 commentary are not represented. A follow-up with browser access would fill this.
