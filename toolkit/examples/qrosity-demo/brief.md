# Director's brief — QrosityDemo (HARNESS §1.2)

```text
CREW: you are the director, writer, animator, composer, sound designer and render engineer.
      Treat this as a multi-session production; don't rush to a final render.
FILM IN ONE LINE: "What if every screen on Earth went black for 10 seconds?" — the world stops,
      then comes back, and you keep scrolling. The viewer should feel the drop, then the twist that
      they're doing it right now.
REFERENCES: none available offline. Grammar from HARNESS §6 + setup/channels/q-rosity/brand.md.
LOOK: night sets built in code (bedroom, street, rooftop skyline, blocky night-side Earth), layered
      depth (foreground props, Q mid, set behind), practical lights (phone, monitor, windows,
      billboards, lamps, moon), fog. Screens die; non-screens (lamps, warm windows, the moon, an ATM
      sign) stay — that contrast is the look. §2 bans named: no dark-navy void with floating bits,
      no glow/bloom/flares, no default fonts, no bounce, no fade-up-everything, no text over the face,
      no "follow for part 2".
CHARACTER BIBLE: Q, the blocky person (same rig as The Signal), Q-ROSITY palette (purple hoodie,
      yellow drawstrings + "?" emblem). Moods: neutral (scrolling) → shocked take ("black?") →
      worried (street, world) → shocked take ("city", "nothing") → worried ("dark") → happy (lights
      back) → neutral (back to scrolling). Poses: phone → shocked → idle → point (city, ATM) → idle → phone.
BEAT SHEET (VO = clock; 15 s):
  0.0 HOOK      frame 0: WHAT IF + premise over Q scrolling in his bedroom, phone-lit face; push-in
  2.4 BLACKOUT  on "black?" (after the one 0.45 s pause) every screen dies: riser → impact, 7 f shake, shocked take
  3.3 10        countdown "10" lands on the spoken "10" (pop); 1 tick per second from here
  4.3 STREET    "Your street goes dark." — the dark wave rolls down the street toward Q (reaches him on "dark")
  5.9 CITY      "Then the entire city." — low angle, rooftop, skyline dies left → right; Q points, shocked take
  7.5 WORLD     "Every plane in the sky." — orbit round a blocky Earth with Q on top; city lights sweep out, planes go dark and tip
  9.2 NOTHING   "No maps, no money, no messages, nothing." — low angle, Q jabs a dead ATM
 11.7 ALONE     "Just you, in the dark." — bust in the dark bedroom, countdown 1
 13.2 LOOP      click: screens back on, light floods his face, "And you keep scrolling." — straight back to the phone; camera returns to frame 0; WHAT IF lands (thump) → last frame = frame 0
TEXT ON SCREEN: big = WHAT IF + premise (hook/loop), the countdown (the story's clock — the only
      number). Captions carry the VO on a plate.
GATES: plan → shot list → stills → draft → critique ×≤3 → audio → final.
DELIVERABLES: out/QrosityDemo.mp4, poster frame (frame 0), critique/rounds/QrosityDemo-round-{1,2,3}.md
```
