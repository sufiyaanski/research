## Chapter one — The first tide (demo: ~45 s; real chapters 90–150 s)

### c01s01 [title]
A few hours after the Moon is gone, the tide comes in. And it's small.

### c01s02 [diagram · orbit]
Tides are mostly the Moon's doing.
Its gravity pulls the oceans into two bulges, and Earth turns through them. That's why most shores get two high tides a day.

### c01s03 [bigNumber · ≈50% on "half"]
The Sun pulls on the oceans too, but only with about half the Moon's force.

### c01s04 [compareBars · 100 vs ≈33]
Take the Moon away, and the tide that's left is roughly a third of what it was.

### c01s05 [narrator · shore · RE-HOOK]
And there's a second change hiding in there.
The biggest tides of the month only happen when the Sun and Moon line up. Without the Moon, every tide is the same small tide.
That sounds harmless. On the shore, it isn't.
