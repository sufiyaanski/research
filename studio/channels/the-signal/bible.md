# The Signal — channel bible

> Read this AND `toolkit/HARNESS.md` (§1–5) before any Signal reel. HARNESS wins on render/timing rules;
> this file wins on what the channel *is*. Edit it when a weekly review changes the strategy.

## 1. Audience & promise
- **Who:** 18–35, curious about money in everyday life, not investors. They shop, scroll, subscribe, tip.
- **Promise (say it in the bio):** "The tricks behind every price you pay — one number at a time."
- **Feeling at the end:** "I'll never see a price tag the same way" → they **send** it to a friend (sends = Instagram's top signal for non-followers).
- **Never:** advice on what to buy/invest in (YouTube bans AI personas giving financial advice). We explain how sellers price, never what you should do with your money.

## 2. Format
- Vertical 1080×1920 @ 30, **45–60 s** master. One mechanism, one surprising number, one everyday sighting.
- Beat grammar: HOOK (0–3 s, number on frame 0) → SETUP → TURN → WHY (name the mechanism) → PROOF (study, one number, source line) → EVERYDAY → PAYOFF (loops to frame 0).
- Platform cuts from the same composition (HARNESS / research 02 §9.1): **IG Reels 50–60 s · TikTok 62–75 s** (extended EVERYDAY beat) **· YT Shorts 35–45 s tight cut** (test vs 55–60 s over time).

## 4. Hook styles (rotate; the weekly review keeps score)
number-first ("This $429 bread maker was never meant to sell") · contradiction ("The cheapest item on the menu is a trap") · "you've been tricked" · question · POV ("POV: you're the person who prices Netflix").

## 5. Look (HARNESS §5 is the law)
- Cream **#F4EFE6** ground, ink **#1B1B1B**, red **#D7263D for money words/objects only**, paper edge #E6DFD2. Nothing else coloured.
- Display Archivo Black / Archivo 800; body Manrope 600; numbers tabular.
- **The blocky character in every beat, ≥ 320 px tall**, `SIGNAL_PALETTE` (ink hoodie). Reacts to numbers; never covers the number or captions.
- Medium: price tags, shelf labels, receipts, tills, phone checkouts on paper with soft contact shadows.

## 6. Banned (on top of HARNESS §2)
Stock footage of shops · dollar-bill rain · "financial freedom" talk · any number without a source line · red on anything that isn't money · "comment below / follow for part 2" outros · hype words (unlock, game-changer, insane).

## 7. Facts
Every number → `studio/episodes/<id>/facts.md` with URL + quote, source line on screen when the number is. Studies are often misquoted: open the paper or the publisher's page, not a blog. No source → no number.

## 8. CTA rules (research 02 §9.5)
- At most **1 in 3 posts** carries a CTA; A/B them via Trial Reels.
- Preferred (reach-safe): *"Send this to the friend who always falls for 'only $9.99'."*
- Lead magnet (IG only, when a cheat sheet exists): *"Comment SIGNAL and I'll DM you the 12-trick cheat sheet."* (one automated DM per comment, within 7 days — Meta rule).
- If CTA posts show lower median non-follower reach than non-CTA posts over 4 weeks → cut to 1 in 5.

## 10. Platform notes
| | Instagram Reels | TikTok | YouTube Shorts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Length | 50–60 s | 62–75 s (Creator Rewards needs > 1 min) | 35–45 s tight cut (test 55–60 s) |
| Caption | 1 line hook + 1 line "why it works" + source credit; ≤ 125 chars before "more" | hook line + question; keep short | title ≤ 60 chars, number-first |
| Hashtags | 0–3 (IG caps at 5; Metricool saw fewer views with hashtags) | 2–4 topical | 1–2 + #Shorts optional |
| Cover | frame 0 (number + character) | frame 0 | frame 0 |
| Music | our synthesised bed only (no licensed tracks: a Content ID claim blocks Shorts > 60 s) | same | same |
| Labels | not required (animated, non-realistic) | not required | not required ("altered or synthetic" is for realistic content) |
| Never | a TikTok-watermarked file anywhere | — | delete + re-upload |
