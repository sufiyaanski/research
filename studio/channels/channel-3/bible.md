## Candidate concepts (ranked — they reuse the existing toolkit; see research/DECISIONS.md)
1. **Scale Shock** — one everyday thing taken to an absurd scale ("Every pizza eaten in the US today, stacked"). Reuses: `bigNumber`/`compareBars` paper templates (re-made vertical), the character standing next to giant objects in 3D, the beat grid. Cheapest to make (mostly 2D), evergreen, highly sendable. Fact risk: low–medium (needs a sourced base number + our arithmetic).
2. **Map Moments** — one geographic oddity per reel ("Why is there a country inside a country?"). Reuses: `diagram` pins → upgrade to a real map with the remotion-maps skill, 3D sets for the on-the-ground beat, character as the traveller. Medium cost; strong search value on YouTube.
3. **Tiny History** — one object's history in 45 s ("The fork took 1,000 years to be accepted"). Reuses: `timeline` + `quote` templates, character in period-flavoured sets. Highest fact risk and needs new sets per era → most expensive; do it last.
