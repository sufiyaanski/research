# channel-3 — idea backlog (template slot)

## Scoring rubric (`/idea` fills it; `/weekly-review` re-scores)
| Column | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook** — would a stranger stop in 2 s? | needs explaining | decent curiosity | impossible to scroll past (number / stakes / contradiction on frame 0) |
| **Novelty** — vs the last 10 episodes (see Used) and the niche | same mechanism/outcome as one of the last 10 | new angle on a used topic | nothing like it in our last 10 |
| **Cost** — production effort (5 = cheap) | new 3D sets + new templates | one new set or component | existing templates/sets only |
| **Fact safety** — risk of being wrong (5 = safe) | contested / medical / legal / needs primary data we cannot get | one sourced number, some interpretation | well-documented, official source |

**Score = 2×Hook + 2×Novelty + Cost + Fact safety** (max 30). Make ≥ 22 first; 18–21 = rework the angle; < 18 = park.
Status: `idea` → `scored` → `picked` (on the calendar) → `in production` (has an episode folder) → `used` (moved to Used).
